What you choose

Pick your combination

Every layer below is independent — mix a base distro, a window management style, and a navigation style however you like.

01

Base Distro

Debian 13, Ubuntu, Linux Mint, LMDE, or Kali — pick the one you already trust from the list above.

02

Window Management

A traditional menu and taskbar, or automatic window tiling — your call.

03

Navigation

Point and click with a mouse, or drive everything from the keyboard with hotkeys.

What you get

Every ingredient, picked with care

Regardless of which base OS you choose, you still get an amazing desktop experience.

01

Cinnamon Desktop

Stable, reliable, and easy to use with a traditional menu and taskbar.

02

Themeable to the Bone

A theme carousel, an Aether theme builder, and a gallery of Omarchy-inspired looks — change your whole desktop's mood in seconds.

03

Containers and VMs, on Tap

Docker, Podman, Distrobox, Boxes, and Virtual Machine Manager — install only the ones you actually use.

AI

An assistant, always in reach

Bring your own agent, reach it with one hotkey, and let it keep working even after you look away.

01

Pick Your Assistant

OpenCode, Claude Code, Pi, and more — install whichever ones you use and set your favorite as default.

02

One Hotkey Away

Super + A always reaches your default assistant, no matter which one it is.

03

Herd Your Agents

Herdr keeps agents running in the background across every project and machine — close the lid, and they're still working when you check back.

Security

Locked down by default

None of this is optional or something you have to remember to turn on — it's already on, out of the box.

01

Firewall by Default

ufw denies all inbound connections out of the box — no ports open unless you open them.

02

Mandatory Access Control

AppArmor is enabled system-wide, confining apps to only the resources they need.

03

Pinned Repositories

Every third-party repo we add is pinned to only the packages it's actually needed for, so it can never silently override the rest of your system.
